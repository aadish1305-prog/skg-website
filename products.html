  • Large cast component machining on our KOLB Double Column VMC

  • Machining of components in steel, cast iron, aluminium alloys, and specialty metals

02

Specialty Material

Precision Machining — Graphite

Specialised machining of graphite components including baffle plates for nuclear power plant cooling towers, graphite trays, and crucible blocks. Our facility features a centralised vacuum dust collection system and dedicated graphite tooling — making us one of the few facilities in the region with this capability.

Graphite Components We Machine

  • Graphite baffle plates — milled from solid graphite plate (chemical and nuclear applications)
  • Graphite trays for metal casting applications
  • Crucible blocks for high-temperature industrial use
  • Segmented graphite fixtures — critical profiling milled from solid graphite block
  • Custom graphite profiles to client drawing and specification

Specialist Facility: Graphite machining requires a dedicated dust management system due to the conductive nature of graphite particles. Our centralised vacuum collection system and isolated machining setup ensure safe, contamination-free production.

03

Aerospace Grade

Precision Machining — Titanium

Titanium is a notoriously difficult material to machine, requiring specialised tooling, controlled cutting parameters and experienced operators. We supply precision-machined titanium components to HAL (Hindustan Aeronautics Limited), meeting strict aerospace-grade tolerances.

Titanium Machining Highlights

  • Precision profile milling of titanium blocks to aerospace-grade dimensional tolerances
  • Dedicated tooling and controlled cutting parameters to manage heat generation
  • Experienced operators trained in handling titanium's unique machining challenges
  • Approved supplier to HAL — Hindustan Aeronautics Limited
  • Full dimensional verification via in-process inspection and external CMM

HAL Approved Supplier: We supply precision-machined titanium blocks and components directly to Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), one of India's premier aerospace organisations — a testament to the precision and reliability our team delivers.

Large Scale Work

Heavy Fabrication

Heavy structural fabrication and machining of large assemblies — including die cooler assemblies, fabricated and machined risers, and large fixture plates — supported by our EOT crane infrastructure for safe handling of heavy components.

Heavy Fabrication Work

  • Die cooler assemblies — fabricated and precision machined steel assemblies with copper cooling blocks
  • Machined and fabricated risers — large fabricated riser structures with precision-machined T-slot surfaces
  • Large fixture plates — welded and machined to tight tolerances for use in production setups
  • Structural machining of large steel weldments and assemblies
  • Components handled up to 7.5 tonnes with our overhead EOT crane infrastructure
