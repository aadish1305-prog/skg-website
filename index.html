Who We Are

50 Years of Engineering Excellence in Nashik

M/s S.K. Gandhi is a Nashik-based heavy engineering and precision machining company established in 1973. We combine decades of hands-on expertise with modern CNC and VMC infrastructure to deliver components and tooling of the highest precision.

Operating from MIDC Satpur, our ISO 9001:2015 certified facility serves clients across automotive diecasting, defence, energy, and specialty material sectors.

Our Core Capabilities

01

Tools, Dies, Jigs & Fixtures

Design and manufacture of precision tools, dies, jigs and fixtures. Machining of client-supplied cast components to tight tolerances across a variety of materials and sizes.

Precision Machining — Graphite

Specialised machining of graphite components including baffle plates, trays and crucible blocks with a centralised vacuum dust collection system and dedicated tooling.
