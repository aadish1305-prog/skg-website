Industry Leaders Who Rely on Us

We are proud to serve some of the most respected names in Indian and global industry. These relationships, built on consistent quality and reliable delivery, span years and in many cases decades.

Graphite India Limited
Graphite & Materials

One of India's leading manufacturers of graphite electrodes and specialty graphite products. We supply precision-machined graphite components including baffle plates and trays for their industrial and chemical applications.

Mahindra & Mahindra
Automotive & Diecasting

A leading Indian multinational automotive manufacturer. We support their diecasting and tooling requirements with precision-machined dies, jigs, fixtures, and cast component machining.

Atlas Copco
Industrial & Energy

A Swedish multinational industrial company specialising in compressors, vacuum solutions, and industrial tools. We supply precision-machined components meeting their exacting dimensional and quality standards.

Bosch
Automotive & Industrial

A global leader in technology and services for mobility, industrial technology, consumer goods, and energy. We machine precision components and tooling to Bosch's rigorous automotive and industrial quality requirements.

ABB
Industrial & Energy

A global technology company leading the digital transformation of industries. We supply precision-machined components for ABB's industrial and energy applications, meeting the dimensional accuracy their systems demand.

HAL — Hindustan Aeronautics Limited
Aerospace & Defence

India's premier state-owned aerospace and defence company, responsible for the design, manufacture, and maintenance of military aircraft. We supply precision-machined titanium components meeting strict aerospace-grade tolerances — one of our most demanding and prestigious supply relationships.

ASB International
Industrial

We supply precision-machined components and tooling to ASB International, supporting their manufacturing and industrial production requirements.

JBM Group
Automotive & Diecasting

A diversified business group with interests in automotive components, renewable energy, and education. We support their automotive diecasting requirements with precision dies, fixtures, and component machining.

Epiroc
Industrial & Mining

A leading productivity partner for the mining, infrastructure, and natural resources industries. We supply precision-machined components meeting the demanding operational requirements of Epiroc's industrial equipment.
